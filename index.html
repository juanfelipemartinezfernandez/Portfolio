The Curious Explorer: My Portfolio

Dear Ignition,

I guess this is where I'm supposed to convince you that I'm the perfect candidate.

I'm not.

I'm just someone who's genuinely curious about how things work.

That curiosity has probably shaped my entire life. One day I'm reading about why earthquakes happen, the next I'm figuring out why an iPhone refuses to send MMS messages, then I'm learning AWS, experimenting with automation, or falling into a rabbit hole about networking, Psychology, or some random topic that absolutely didn't need two hours of my attention... but got them anyway.

I've spent years in customer support and technical support, and somewhere along the way I realized my favorite part wasn't fixing computers or phones—it was helping another human go from "Nothing works." to "You are my hero."

I like that moment.

Outside of work, I'm probably what people would call a serial learner. I enjoy programming, cloud technologies, AI, psychology, finance, and whatever else catches my attention that week. Sometimes my browser has 40 tabs open because I convinced myself every single one was important.

(They weren't.)

One thing that's important to me is being comfortable being wrong. If someone shows me a better way to do something, I don't see that as losing an argument—I see it as getting smarter. I'd rather improve than protect my ego.

Favorite joke?

I probably don't have one in English—I do in Spanish. Although, while looking online, I found one that actually made me laugh.

Why do programmers prefer dark mode?

Because light attracts bugs.

I know... I deserve that.

Now, about AI.

Yes, I used AI to help with this letter.

But probably not in the way you're trying to filter out.

I use AI every day as a thinking partner, not a ghostwriter. I ask it to challenge my assumptions, help me prepare for interviews, summarize long documentation, brainstorm troubleshooting paths, improve my writing, and occasionally tell me when I'm confidently wrong. Then I rewrite things until they actually sound like me.

This letter started as my ideas and experiences. AI helped organize them, tighten the writing, and remove the awkward sentences that somehow always survive my first draft. If anything here feels authentic, that's because the stories are mine. AI just helped me tell them better.

Finally, here's something that probably explains me better than any résumé.

If I don't know something today, I don't get discouraged.

I get excited.

Because tomorrow I probably will.

Thanks for reading this far. Hopefully we get the chance to have a real conversation.

— Juan Felipe Martinez
